# THE UNDERCARD — Hearn v White

Original fictional comedy animation. This is an invented event and invented dialogue, not reporting a real ban or confirmed fight. Approximate duration: 4 minutes. Landscape 1920×1080 master; keep action inside centre 60% for later portrait crops. Use the supplied cartoon style, heightened expressions and closed slit mouths. No gore. Fury and Joshua are the straight men; the promoters are the idiots.

## 1. The ban — 0:00–0:35
Backstage press area. Eddie stands beside a printed picture of Dana with a red cross over it. Add all text in the engine; never rely on image-generated lettering.

EDDIE [polished, pleased with himself]: Tonight is about two elite heavyweights. Not a bald American turning up and calling everybody a pussy.
SECURITY [dry]: So Mr White's banned?
EDDIE [firm]: Completely. No ticket. No guest list. No exceptions.
SECURITY: And if he buys a ticket?
EDDIE: Especially then. I don't want him enjoying himself at my expense.
Cut outside to Dana studying the loading bay.
DANA [irritated]: He banned me? From boxing? Half the people in there don't even like boxing. They're there because their table cost twelve grand.
Laundry trolley passes. Dana looks at it, then at camera. Hold 12 frames before decision.
DANA [matter-of-fact]: I've entered venues in worse ways.
Smash cut: his polished shoe disappears under towels.

## 2. The world's angriest towel — 0:35–1:10
Low tracking shot beside trolley. Wheels squeak; one wheel wobbles. Laundry attendant remains utterly bored.

ATTENDANT: American machine. Makes a noise.

DANA [whispering]: Easy. Easiest entrance I've ever—
Trolley hits floor cable protector. Freeze on Dana's eyes widening, then three rapid tumble poses. Trolley does NOT strike Fury; Dana lands at his feet with a towel over his head.

## 6. The fight — 2:50–3:40
Bell. Silent 3-second face-off. Both shuffle, nobody punches. Crowd cough. Ringside Tyson impatient.
TYSON: Go on! It's not a podcast!
Eddie attempts long jab. Dana leans back much too far, windmills arms, recovers. Eddie's jab misses; do not show contact. Dana throws short jab; Eddie turns away and shields face with both gloves. One glove gently bumps guard with rubber squeak.
EDDIE [outraged]: You actually fucking hit me!
DANA: What did you think this was?
EDDIE: A commercial opportunity!
Three exchanges: jab/dodge; simultaneous overcommitted punches miss; both end up in clinch, exhausted, hanging on each other. Each move needs wind-up, extension, recovery; feet planted during strike. No rapid sprite slideshow.
DANA [breathless]: You tired?
EDDIE [breathless denial]: I'm letting it breathe. Builds demand.
REFEREE: Break!
They reluctantly separate, immediately wobble and lean together again.
JOSHUA: They're hugging more than they're fighting.
TYSON: That's the first deal they've agreed on.
Eddie whispers while clinched.
EDDIE: Rematch. Fifty-fifty.
DANA: Sixty-forty.
EDDIE: You came in a bin.
DANA: And sold the fucking fight.
Bell. Both raise arms as if they won. Ref looks at blank scorecard.

Attendant rolls both bickering voices offscreen. End on Fury/Joshua staring. Wheel squeak provides final beat. Cut to black before anyone adds a last joke.

## Direction and animation rules
Let eyes and pauses carry the jokes. Hold a reaction 8–16 frames after punchline; avoid a cut on every word. Press scene composed like serious sporting coverage; bin entrance shifts to low physical-comedy angles; fight returns to dramatic lights over pathetic action.

Rig characters from supplied reference sprites where possible: head, brows, eyes, torso, upper/lower arms, hands/gloves, thighs, shins, feet. These generated sheets are source illustrations, not already rigged puppets or guaranteed frame-perfect walk cycles. Separate parts cleanly and repair hidden joints before production. Never rotate an entire flat body to mimic walking. Never squash heads. Close mouth slit belongs on lower face and must be masked/replaced before speech. Preserve beard edge around animated mouth.

Align recorded voice with Whisper or existing repository aligner. Use phonemes/word timings, not uniform mouth toggles. Rest mouth on pauses. No open mouth artwork is needed from this pack. Blinks 2–4 frames with staggered timing. Draw closed lids if source sheet lacks them. Eye direction follows scene target. Keep brows out of eyeballs.

Prop contacts: mic gripped, trolley handles gripped, gloves on wrist anchors, towels occluding body correctly. Laundry lid, towels, trolley body and wheels should be independent layers. Ring near ropes must pass in front of fighters; far ropes behind them. Crowd, canvas, ropes, posts, lights and haze need separate compositing layers where practical.

Screen direction: Eddie usually screen left facing right; Dana right facing left. Both travel left-to-right into backstage. Fury tall and broad; Joshua athletic; Eddie tall and narrow; Dana shorter and stockier. Flip only text-free symmetric sprites; preserve glove colour and outfit identity. Boxing stance turns naturally and changes feet; mirror is a fallback, not a complete second rig.

Audio: clean dialogue centred; machinery low; trolley wheel squeak, towel rustle, shoe scuff, boxing bell, glove squeak and breathing are distinct cues. Dip crowd under dialogue. No constant music across jokes. Two-second dramatic sting before announcer reveal. Fight music stops at first embarrassing glove tap.

First build: animatic using temporary voice timing, then aligned 720p preview. Deliver preview before final 4K. Inspect foot sliding, face placement, mouth mask, prop grips, rope layering, dropped frame gaps, audible words and joke pauses. Do not use Runway. Use user's existing repository engine when provided; this pack does not include or claim access to that engine.
