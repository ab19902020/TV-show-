# Rio, the Beg Friend

Rio Ferdinand defends his old Leeds team-mate Jason Wilcox, and can't stop sucking up to him: tea and biscuits,
a bow, shoulder rubs, a thumbs up. Wilcox basks in it. Remade from the earlier cutout version with the same audio.

**[`Rio_Beg_Friend_1080x1920.mp4`](Rio_Beg_Friend_1080x1920.mp4)**: 51 s, portrait 1080x1920.
[`Rio_Beg_Friend_share.mp4`](Rio_Beg_Friend_share.mp4) is a 720p copy for sending.

- **Whole drawings** from the character sheet (no loose heads or arms): Rio with the tray, serving, three shoulder
  rubs, five gestures; Wilcox in his chair, smug, blissful, eyes up, shrugging. Placed at true scale in the office,
  behind the desk, with the tea staying on the desk once served.
- **Lip sync from the audio**: Rio's clip cut into eight lines, force-aligned word by word; his mouth opens on the
  sounds, he blinks, looks and nods on the stressed words.
- **Captions** word by word (the current word in yellow), the title strip, floating hearts and a soft string pad
  under the shoulder rubs, and the BEST MATES end card.
