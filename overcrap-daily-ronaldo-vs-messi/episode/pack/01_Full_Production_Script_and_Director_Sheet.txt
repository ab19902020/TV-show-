THE OVERCRAP DAILY – Ronaldo vs Messi
FULL PRODUCTION SCRIPT & DIRECTOR PACK


OPENING - straight in, no intro
DIRECTOR: Camera begins on a medium/wide studio shot with Mark already mid-rant. Mark uses strong hand gestures. Rooney looks genuinely curious; Roy remains almost motionless. Hold reaction beats after 'Darren' and 'Depends on Darren'. No literal wedding or dog/studio gag is required; keep the studio grounded.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [animated] Messi and Ronaldo right now are like two blokes leaving the same wedding. Messi's gone at half eleven, hugged the bride's nan, thanked the parents, ordered an Uber and everyone's waving him off.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [building] Ronaldo's still there at four in the morning, shirt unbuttoned, arguing with the DJ because he's stopped playing his song, telling the best man the wedding wouldn't have happened without him.

WAYNE ROONEY: [curious] What song's he asking for?

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [annoyed] That's not the point, Wayne.

ROY KEANE: [dry] It probably is if he's been arguing for four hours.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [animated] One's left like a national treasure. The other's thirty seconds from being removed by a bloke called Darren in a high-vis jacket.

WAYNE ROONEY: [thoughtful] I reckon Darren struggles.

ROY KEANE: [serious] Depends on Darren.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [frustrated] Can we stop scouting the imaginary bouncer?


RONALDO / PORTUGAL DISCUSSION
DIRECTOR: Keep this section snappy and believable as football analysis. Use two-shots and reaction close-ups rather than cutting every line. Roy is deadpan; Rooney is balanced; Mark becomes more animated around DEFCON One.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [serious] Messi gets this huge emotional goodbye. Ronaldo falls out with Portugal and suddenly it's a national emergency.

ROY KEANE: [firm] The manager picks the team.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [agreeing] Exactly.

WAYNE ROONEY: [serious] If Ronaldo thinks he's been promised something, he's going to be annoyed.

ROY KEANE: [blunt] Be annoyed. Get on with it.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [animated] That's the thing with Ronaldo. He doesn't get dropped. Portugal goes to DEFCON One.

WAYNE ROONEY: [matter-of-fact] He's always wanted to play every minute.

ROY KEANE: [serious] That mentality made him great.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [emphatic] Fine, but somebody has to be able to say, 'Cristiano, you're on the bench,' without calling in the army.

WAYNE ROONEY: [dry] I'd let someone else tell him.

ROY KEANE: [dry] Coward.

WAYNE ROONEY: [challenging] You tell him.

ROY KEANE: [firm] I would.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [dry] Of course you bloody would.


MESSI SEND-OFF
DIRECTOR: Play the silence/reaction after Roy's 'They'll see him again.' Mark should look as if he is trying to explain emotion to a wall. Rooney's 'Fair' is tiny and understated.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [animated] Then Messi gets what looks like a state funeral while he's still alive.

ROY KEANE: [confused] Why are people crying?

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [frustrated] Because he's leaving!

ROY KEANE: [matter-of-fact] They'll see him again.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [exasperated] Roy, could you allow one emotion into the programme?

ROY KEANE: [flat] No.

WAYNE ROONEY: [agreeing] Fair.


RIO ENTERS
DIRECTOR: Rio is not present before this beat. His entrance is the episode's largest visual change. Widen the shot as he enters, then use tighter coverage. Rio is sincere, not knowingly comedic.

RIO FERDINAND: [interrupting, animated] Whoa, whoa, whoa. Hold on.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [sigh] Oh, here we go.

RIO FERDINAND: [animated] I've been listening outside.

WAYNE ROONEY: [curious] Why were you outside?

RIO FERDINAND: [matter-of-fact] I wasn't booked.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [dry] You're still not.

RIO FERDINAND: [serious] I heard Cristiano's name.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [teasing] And your Ronaldo alarm went off.

RIO FERDINAND: [passionate] You lot are talking about one of the greatest players EVER.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [defensive] Nobody said he wasn't.

RIO FERDINAND: [accusing] I heard the tone.

ROY KEANE: [suspicious] What tone?

RIO FERDINAND: [serious] The tone.


FOREST FAN RUNNING JOKE
DIRECTOR: This is a major comedy beat. Use a brief pause after Rio says 'Forest fan anyway'. Rooney visibly laughs. Mark's gestures and face become much bigger while Rio stays calm. Do not overextend it.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [trying to interrupt] Rio, all I said was the manager has to be allowed to—

RIO FERDINAND: [interrupting] Mark, hold on.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [interrupting] But—

RIO FERDINAND: [firm] Hold on.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [frustrated] Rio—

RIO FERDINAND: [annoyed] Shut up. You're a Forest fan anyway.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [angry] OH, FOR GOD'S SAKE.

WAYNE ROONEY: [laughs]

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [shouting] I AM NOT A FOREST FAN.

RIO FERDINAND: [matter-of-fact] You're from Nottingham.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [frustrated] That doesn't make me a Forest fan!

ROY KEANE: [dry] You're very defensive.

RIO FERDINAND: [teasing] Exactly what a Forest fan would say.


RIO DEFENDS RONALDO / HAM
DIRECTOR: Keep Rio completely sincere. Rooney genuinely wants an answer. After 'Iberico', give Rooney a small satisfied reaction before Mark explodes.

RIO FERDINAND: [passionate] Cristiano's won everything. Champions Leagues. Ballon d'Ors. Records. Goals. Standards.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [exasperated] Yes, Rio, we know who Ronaldo is!

RIO FERDINAND: [serious] If Cristiano tells me he should be playing, I'm listening.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [sarcastic] You'd listen if Cristiano told you the moon was made of ham.

RIO FERDINAND: [completely serious] I'd check.

WAYNE ROONEY: [genuinely curious] What kind of ham?

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [shouting] WAYNE.

RIO FERDINAND: [thinking] Iberico.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [baffled] Why are we discussing lunar meat?!


ENDING
DIRECTOR: Increase pace slightly. Mark briefly thinks he has won after Rio says 'Of course'. Hard cut immediately after GOODNIGHT. No outro scene.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [calm, challenging] Simple question. Could Ronaldo ever be wrong?

RIO FERDINAND: [calm] Of course.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [relieved] Thank you.

RIO FERDINAND: [confident] Just not about football.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [immediately annoyed] Oh, piss off.

ROY KEANE: [amused] He walked into that.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [defeated] So Messi gets the perfect goodbye, Ronaldo gets chaos, and apparently I support Nottingham Forest.

RIO FERDINAND: [smug] Finally, some facts.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [warning] RIO.

RIO FERDINAND: [teasing] Forest.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [frustrated] We're done.

WAYNE ROONEY: [innocent] What happened to Darren?

ROY KEANE: [deadpan] Still trying to get Ronaldo out the wedding.

RIO FERDINAND: [firm] He's not getting Cristiano out.

MARK GOLDBRIDGE: [shouting] GOODNIGHT.
