# Backgrounds

These are the background plates and canvas images for the animated documentary
parody. They live in [`assets/backgrounds/`](assets/backgrounds/), in one folder
per location. Every image is a 1672 × 941 (about 16:9) opaque RGB PNG, painted
in a red-and-black club palette with inked outlines to sit behind the
house-style characters.

| Location | Images | Suggested use |
|---|---|---|

| [`boardroom/`](assets/backgrounds/boardroom/) | [club boardroom](assets/backgrounds/boardroom/cinematic-football-club-boardroom.png) · [executive boardroom at sunset](assets/backgrounds/boardroom/crimson-executive-boardroom-at-sunset.png) | Owners and executives meetings |

The images are unmodified originals from the *Animated TV Show Backgrounds*
upload. The file names are the originals in kebab case. Parts 1 and 3 of 3 are
included; add Part 2 here when it's available.

Before compositing, check that the eye line and floor perspective suit the
character scale. Add foreground occluders, such as desk fronts and chair backs,
as separate cut-out layers if characters need to sit behind them.
