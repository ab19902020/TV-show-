# Backgrounds

These are the background plates and canvas images for the animated documentary
parody. They live in [`assets/backgrounds/`](assets/backgrounds/), in one folder
per location. There are 26 images in total. Every image is a 1672 × 941 (about
16:9) opaque RGB PNG, painted in a red-and-black club palette with inked
outlines to sit behind the house-style characters.

The images are unmodified originals from all three parts of the *Animated TV
Show Backgrounds* upload. The file names are the originals in kebab case.

Before compositing, check that the eye line and floor perspective suit the
character scale. Add foreground occluders, such as desk fronts, chair backs and
bench seats, as separate cut-out layers if characters need to sit behind them.
