## Character performance

Flashback characters do not mouth every word of Evra's narration. They act silently under it.
If a directly quoted line is deliberately staged as spoken by a flashback character, lip-sync only that recorded line. Otherwise use neutral, breathing, smile and reaction mouths.
The face sheets provide REST, A, E, I, O, U, M/B/P and F/V plus eyes and blinks. They are image reference atlases: crop and mask each intended patch, align it to the head, and erase or cover the original mouth before placing a replacement.
Never paste a second lower face over a visible existing face. Eye strips also require masking; their rectangular backgrounds must not appear in the shot.
Blink irregularly and sparingly. Let eye-lines and short still holds deliver the comedy.

## Compositing and movement

These PNG backgrounds are flattened plates, not layered PSD files. Extract foreground table, pool edge or bench masks during scene assembly where occlusion is needed.
Characters seated at lunch must sit behind the table, with hands and fork correctly above it. Never show an extra plate already baked into the background plus another on top.
Swimming characters need a waterline mask. Sauna characters need a bench contact point.
Change boots to trainers before the house scenes. Change both characters to swim trunks for the pool/jacuzzi/sauna; do not alternate outfits between cuts.
A foot stays fixed during its contact phase. Animate the pose or a proper cutout rig rather than stretching the entire raster.
Keep one shoe attached to each foot. Rotate the football independently and time contact to the kicking foot.
Keep utensils in the correct hand. No detached fingers, duplicated faces or floating props.
Use subtle camera pushes and decisive cuts. Keep lighting and character exposure stable.
