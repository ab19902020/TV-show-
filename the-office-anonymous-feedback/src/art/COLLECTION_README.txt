THE OFFICE CARTOON ANIMATION COLLECTION

David Brent is the goateed manager. The three formerly mislabelled sheets are preserved as David_Brent_Alternate.

Characters: David Brent, Tim Canterbury, Dawn Tinsley, Gareth Keenan, Finchy, Neil Godwin.
Each character has 24 new full-body action poses with a simple closed mouth, six previous views and eight previous walk frames. No detached limbs. These are still pose assets, not rendered animations.
Backgrounds are empty landscape cartoon sets, roughly 16:9, around 1672 x 941 pixels. They are stylised interpretations rather than exact set replicas.
New action sprites are extracted at native sheet resolution (about 256 x 256 each); original full sheets are included. Some generated sprites retain a soft surrounding halo and may need matte cleanup before final compositing. Source pixels and alpha are preserved. Some seated poses are floor-sitting or reclining; use the upright bent-knee poses for office chairs.

CLAUDE: use the new Actions poses as acting keys, animate transitions and create mouth movement from each simple mouth. Keep body identity and outfit consistent. Keep full legs/torso present behind desk occlusion. Put each desk foreground in front of a seated character as a mask; do not erase the character torso. Anchor shoes to the floor for walks. Walk frames are source references and require timing/alignment checks. Do not animate detached parts or stretch sprites.

Original_Sheets preserves earlier files, including legacy vowel sheets for completeness; they are not required for the new workflow.

See asset_index.csv for individual PNG names and native sizes. prompts.json records the built-in image generation prompts.
