# ANONYMOUS FEEDBACK
## Original fan sketch — complete animation brief

**Cast:** David Brent, Gareth Keenan, Tim Canterbury. **Length:** approximately 1 minute 55 seconds to 2 minutes. **Format:** landscape 16:9, 1920 × 1080, 25 fps. Dialogue below is new, not taken from an existing episode. No narrator.

**Premise:** Brent introduces anonymous staff suggestions to look approachable on camera. Gareth treats anonymity as a security problem. One mildly critical suggestion turns Brent's listening exercise into an investigation.

### Performance and comedy

Keep everything small and believable. They are at work; they do not know they are delivering punchlines. The embarrassment comes from people defending their own behaviour, and from the camera staying after they wish it would leave.

- **Brent:** wants the crew to witness a popular, progressive manager. Starts with relaxed, rehearsed confidence. Criticism makes his smile stay fixed while his eyes become suspicious. He corrects the meaning of words to protect himself. Allow tiny false starts and changes of tack; never make him shout or become a pantomime villain.
- **Gareth:** genuinely believes procedures make him indispensable. Upright, earnest and slightly defensive. His investigation is entirely serious. He watches Brent for approval after offering information. No knowing smiles to the audience.
- **Tim:** appears to be doing his job. His questions sound reasonable and curious. He enjoys giving the other two just enough rope. Keep his delivery quiet, with minimal eyebrow movement. Only one clear look into the lens; save it for the ending.

No canned laughter, comedy music, exaggerated zooms, bouncing idle animations or constant eyebrow waggling. Use soft fluorescent hum, occasional distant keys and a paper rustle. Silence is part of the scene.

### Timed shooting script

Times are edit targets. Record the dialogue naturally first, then adjust holds to land between 115 and 120 seconds. Do not accelerate the voices to hit exact timestamps.

#### SHOT 01 — 00:00–00:07 — Office two-shot, already in progress

Tim sits left, one hand near the keyboard. Gareth sits right with the shoebox on his desk. Brent stands beside Gareth, presenting the box with a small open-palm gesture. No entrance walk: begin after he has arrived.

**BRENT — B01** *(casual confidence; making it sound spontaneous)*  
“Little initiative. Anonymous suggestions. Anything you think management could improve.”

**Face/action:** Brent gives the documentary crew a quick, pleased glance on “management,” then looks back at the men. Tim stops typing. Gareth straightens.

#### SHOT 02 — 00:07–00:21 — Brent interview

Cut to Brent in the interview chair, looking just off-camera at the unseen interviewer. Start with a modest smile; hands resting, then one small explanatory gesture.

**BRENT — B02** *(warm; self-congratulatory, then carefully qualifying)*  
“People can be completely honest with me. That's the relationship. Obviously, if it's personal, that's not feedback. That's just… having a go.”

**Hold:** leave roughly one second after “having a go.” His smile asks for agreement. Nobody supplies it. Cut before he can explain again.

#### SHOT 03 — 00:21–00:29 — Back at the desks

Gareth taps his notebook once with the pen. Tim turns only his eyes toward Gareth.

**GARETH — G01** *(helpful; proud of being organised)*  
“I've numbered the slips. And kept a list of who had which number.”

#### SHOT 04 — 00:29–00:34 — Tim single

**TIM — T01** *(gently interested; no sarcasm in the voice)*  
“So what makes them anonymous?”

**Face/action:** slight brow lift. He looks at Gareth, not at the camera.

#### SHOT 05 — 00:34–00:40 — Gareth single

**GARETH — G02** *(patient explanation of something obvious)*  
“No names on the actual slip.”

Gareth looks down at his notebook, satisfied. Hold his serious face for a short beat after the line.

#### SHOT 06 — 00:40–00:50 — Medium on Brent and Gareth

Brent takes the folded suggestion slip from the box. Keep the handling simple: fingers close on paper, paper moves with the hand, hand stops before the line begins. Gareth watches him read.

**BRENT — B03** *(bright at first; slightly less pleased after reading)*  
“Right. First one. ‘Fewer meetings.’ Fair enough. Constructive. Who put that?”

**Face/action:** the smile drops only a little on “Fewer meetings.” He restores it for “Constructive.” “Who put that?” should sound conversational, as if he has forgotten his own rule.

#### SHOT 07 — 00:50–00:56 — Tim single

**TIM — T02** *(helpful reminder)*  
“I thought we weren't meant to know.”

He lets his hand settle on the desk. No smirk.

#### SHOT 08 — 00:56–01:06 — Brent medium

**BRENT — B04** *(mildly defensive; immediately reframing)*  
“We aren't. I'm asking if anyone wants to take credit. It's a good suggestion.”

Brent holds the paper a little higher, as if its goodness proves his openness. His eyes move between Tim and Gareth, waiting for someone to identify themselves. Allow one short unanswered beat.

#### SHOT 09 — 01:06–01:19 — Gareth, then Tim; cut only on the turn

**GARETH — G03** *(quietly pleased with his evidence)*  
“Number four. Tim.”

Cut to Tim.

**TIM — T03** *(calm; still addressing Gareth)*  
“Could you put ‘stop Gareth keeping a list’ on mine as well?”

Cut back to Gareth, already beginning to write.

**GARETH — G04** *(matter-of-fact administration)*  
“That'll need a separate slip.”

**Face/action:** Gareth actually checks the notebook before answering. Tim's response is a tiny exhale, not laughter. Leave a little space after “separate slip.”

#### SHOT 10 — 01:19–01:30 — Brent and desk two-shot

Brent folds the suggestion once, neatly. This physical tidying is his attempt to regain control. Gareth stops writing to listen.

**BRENT — B05** *(reasonable managerial tone; sees no contradiction)*  
“Tell you what. Get everyone together after lunch. Discuss why people feel we're having too many meetings.”

#### SHOT 11 — 01:30–01:43 — Tim, then Brent

**TIM — T04** *(checking a practical detail)*  
“In a meeting?”

Cut to Brent. Hold half a second before he answers.

**BRENT — B06** *(quiet correction; restrained irritation)*  
“Not a meeting. A chance to speak freely.”

His chin lifts slightly. He thinks he has settled it.

#### SHOT 12 — 01:43–02:00 — Final exchange and reaction

Gareth raises the pen a few centimetres, ready to note attendance.

**GARETH — G05** *(genuine procedural question)*  
“Is it compulsory?”

**BRENT — B07** *(immediate, ordinary certainty)*  
“Yes.”

Leave a one-second gap. Tim looks at Brent, then at the box.

**TIM — T05** *(mild; testing the advertised principle)*  
“Can we vote on that anonymously?”

**BRENT — B08** *(briskly accommodating; genuinely thinks this works)*  
“Yeah. Show of hands.”

**Ending:** hold the wide for one second. Nobody moves. Cut to Tim; his eyes move to the lens, followed by a very small, exhausted half-smile. Hold two seconds with office hum. Hard cut to black. No wink, sting, caption or extra punchline.
