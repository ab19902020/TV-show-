Carrington. The beating heart of Manchester United.

A place built on hard work, on loyalty, and on a deep, unshakeable hatred of Leeds.

But one man believes the club has a mole.

In twenty nineteen, Leeds were caught spying on another club's training ground.

Lisandro considers this a tutorial.

Day one of the surveillance operation.

At two fifty-eight, the director of football locked his door.

At two fifty-nine, he closed the blinds.

And at three o'clock... he let it breathe.

The mole had been found. The plant did not survive.

Mr Wilcox requested a right of reply.

The presentation lasted seventy minutes.

Omar Berrada joined United from Manchester City. Lisandro had not done the reading.

That night, Lisandro Martinez believed he was the last loyal man at Carrington.
