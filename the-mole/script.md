# ALL OR SOMETHING — THE MOLE

**An All or Something episode: a fake fly-on-the-wall documentary.** Lisandro Martinez is convinced Manchester United has a mole, and he is sure it is the director of football, Jason Wilcox. He goes undercover (in a hedge, then in a pot plant). At three o'clock Wilcox locks his door, unzips his United top to show a Leeds shirt underneath, and refreshes the Leeds live page on BBC Sport. Licho bursts out of the plant: "I knew it!" Then it gets worse for everybody.

**Speaking cast:** NARRATOR (deep, serious documentary voice), LISANDRO (Lisandro "Licho" Martinez), WILCOX (Jason Wilcox). Omar Berrada appears but does not speak. All dialogue and events are invented parody.

**Timing guide:** about three and a half minutes. The 63 lines have 470 spoken words, about 3:10 at 150 words per minute, plus about 25 seconds for the silent gags (the unzip, the soil explosion, the long silences). The recordings decide the real length. Director notes in italics are not spoken.

**The real things the jokes hang on:** Wilcox played for Blackburn (they won the 1994-95 league one point ahead of United), then Leeds (1999-2004). After that he ran Manchester City's academy, then he was Southampton's director of football. In 2019 Leeds were caught spying on Derby's training ground ("Spygate"), and their manager Marcelo Bielsa answered with a 70-minute presentation. Omar Berrada came to United from City Football Group. Alexis Mac Allister is Licho's World Cup-winning Argentina teammate, and he plays for Liverpool.

## SCENE 1 — THE HEDGE

**Guide time: 00:00–00:20.**

**EXT. CARRINGTON TRAINING GROUND. DAWN.**

*Slow drone push over the training ground at dawn. Mist, sprinklers, serious strings: the full documentary opening. The red-framed glass entrance.*

[L001] NARRATOR: Carrington. The beating heart of Manchester United.
[L002] NARRATOR: A place built on hard work, on loyalty, and on a deep, unshakeable hatred of Leeds.

*Tilt down to the hedge by the car park. The hedge has eyes. And binoculars. And a metal straw: someone in there is drinking mate.*

[L003] NARRATOR: But one man believes the club has a mole.

*The binoculars turn slowly to the camera. Licho's face is smeared with camouflage paint.*

[L004] LISANDRO: Shhh. I am not here.

*A long, loud slurp of mate from inside the hedge. TITLE CARD: ALL OR SOMETHING. Then: THE MOLE.*

## SCENE 2 — THE EVIDENCE

**Guide time: 00:20–01:00.**

**INT. INTERVIEW ROOM. DAY.**

*Talking head. Licho sits in the documentary chair, still in camo paint, with a twig in his hair. LOWER THIRD: LISANDRO MARTINEZ — DEFENDER. Behind him is a conspiracy board: red string, photos of Jason Wilcox, a white mug in an evidence bag, and a map of Yorkshire with a circle round Leeds.*

[L005] LISANDRO: I am a defender. My job is to smell danger before it happens.
[L006] LISANDRO: And this building smells of Leeds.

*Whip pan to the board. On each club, a photo of young Wilcox in that kit slams up.*

[L007] LISANDRO: Jason Wilcox. Director of football. Look at his career.
[L008] LISANDRO: Blackburn. He won the league against United.
[L009] LISANDRO: Leeds. I don't need to explain.
[L010] LISANDRO: Manchester City.

*He has to stop for a moment. He composes himself.*

[L011] LISANDRO: Southampton. Okay, Southampton is fine.
[L012] LISANDRO: This is not a CV. This is a criminal record.
[L013] LISANDRO: Every day, he drinks his tea from a white mug. Why white, Jason? Why always white?
[L014] LISANDRO: When United score, he claps like this.

*One limp, polite clap. Dead eyes.*

[L015] LISANDRO: When Leeds score, I hear him in the toilet. Screaming.
[L016] NARRATOR: In twenty nineteen, Leeds were caught spying on another club's training ground.
[L017] NARRATOR: Lisandro considers this a tutorial.

*Licho slowly paints a second camo stripe under each eye, holding eye contact with the lens.*

## SCENE 3 — THE OPERATION

**Guide time: 01:00–01:45.**

**INT. CARRINGTON. CORRIDOR OUTSIDE THE DIRECTOR OF FOOTBALL'S OFFICE. DAY.**

*A big pot plant stands by a glass door marked DIRECTOR OF FOOTBALL. The plant has trainers. Every time someone walks past it shuffles an inch, then stops dead when they look.*

[L018] NARRATOR: Day one of the surveillance operation.
[L019] LISANDRO: Target is in the office. Target is drinking tea. White mug. Of course.

**INT. WILCOX'S OFFICE. CONTINUOUS.**

*Wilcox is at his desk in his zipped black United training top, on the phone: every inch the modern football executive.*

[L020] WILCOX: Absolutely. We're fully aligned. Red through and through. One club, one vision, one process.

*He hangs up and looks at the door. He gets up.*

[L021] NARRATOR: At two fifty-eight, the director of football locked his door.

*Click.*

[L022] NARRATOR: At two fifty-nine, he closed the blinds.

*Behind him, in the corner of the office, stands the pot plant. It is already in. Nobody knows how.*

[L023] NARRATOR: And at three o'clock... he let it breathe.

*Slow motion. Smooth saxophone. Wilcox slowly unzips his United top. Underneath is a pristine white Leeds shirt. He lets out a long sigh of relief, like a man taking his shoes off after a wedding. He opens his laptop: BBC SPORT — LIVE: LEEDS UNITED. He refreshes. Refreshes. Leans in.*

[L024] WILCOX: Come on, Leeds. Come on, lads.

*In the corner, the plant's leaves part. Two eyes, wide.*

[L025] LISANDRO: Madre mia.

*BBC live text: GOAL! Wilcox explodes out of his chair, both arms up.*

[L026] WILCOX: Yes! Get in! Marching on together!

*The plant EXPLODES. Soil everywhere. Licho rises out of the pot like a swamp monster, leaves in his hair, pointing.*

[L027] LISANDRO: I knew it!

*Wilcox screams. Licho screams. Wilcox screams again because Licho screamed. FREEZE FRAME.*

[L028] NARRATOR: The mole had been found. The plant did not survive.

## SCENE 4 — BUSTED

**Guide time: 01:45–02:25.**

**INT. WILCOX'S OFFICE. CONTINUOUS.**

*Wilcox frantically tries to zip the top back up. The zip is stuck. He holds a cushion over the badge.*

[L029] WILCOX: Lisandro! This isn't what it looks like.
[L030] LISANDRO: It looks like Leeds.
[L031] WILCOX: It's scouting. Data-led opposition scouting. I'm immersing myself in the enemy.
[L032] LISANDRO: In their shirt?
[L033] WILCOX: Method scouting. It's very modern. You wouldn't understand, you were in a plant.
[L034] LISANDRO: Turn around.

*Wilcox turns slowly. On the back: WILCOX. And in black marker: "To Jason. Love, Jason. x"*

[L035] LISANDRO: You signed your own shirt?
[L036] WILCOX: It was a difficult time for me.

*PING. The laptop. Wilcox's eyes flick to it. He cannot stop himself.*

[L037] WILCOX: Oh, that's never a penalty, ref!

*He looks up. Licho is just pointing at him. Long hold.*

[L038] LISANDRO: Say it.
[L039] WILCOX: Say what?
[L040] LISANDRO: Leeds are rubbish.

*Wilcox straightens his collar and opens his mouth. Nothing comes out. Sweat. His eye twitches. It is like watching a vampire try to eat garlic.*

[L041] WILCOX: Leeds are... really well coached, to be fair.

*Licho turns to the documentary camera, devastated.*

[L042] LISANDRO: He cannot say it. He is sick.

## SCENE 5 — THE RIGHT OF REPLY

**Guide time: 02:25–02:55.**

**INT. CARRINGTON BOARDROOM. DAY.**

*The tactics screen now shows a PowerPoint: WHY I AM DEFINITELY NOT A LEEDS FAN. Slide 1 of 214. Wilcox has a laser pointer and is back in his United top, zipped to the chin. Licho is the only person in the audience.*

[L043] NARRATOR: Mr Wilcox requested a right of reply.
[L044] WILCOX: Slide one. My commitment to Manchester United, as a percentage. Up eleven percent on last season.

*A bar chart. One bar. No axis.*

[L045] LISANDRO: Up from what?
[L046] WILCOX: We don't need to get into the baseline.
[L047] NARRATOR: The presentation lasted seventy minutes.

*Licho is asleep in his chair. Behind him: SLIDE 213 — AND ANOTHER THING.*

**INT. INTERVIEW ROOM. DAY.** *Talking head.*

[L048] LISANDRO: Seventy minutes of PowerPoint. You know who else does this? Bielsa. Leeds. Case closed.

**INT. WILCOX'S OFFICE. DAY.** *Talking head. LOWER THIRD: JASON WILCOX — DIRECTOR OF FOOTBALL. Zipped to the chin. The soil has been hoovered up, mostly.*

[L049] WILCOX: I bleed red. I always have.
[L050] WILCOX: There might be a bit of white in there. That's medical.

## SCENE 6 — GOING TO THE TOP

**Guide time: 02:55–03:15.**

**INT. INTERVIEW ROOM. DAY.**

[L051] LISANDRO: So I go to the top. Omar will know what to do.

**INT. CEO'S OFFICE. DAY.**

*Licho bursts in.*

[L052] LISANDRO: Omar! We have a mole!

*Omar Berrada sits at his desk in a sky-blue Manchester City scarf, holding a City mug, with City highlights on his monitor. He freezes mid-sip. Silence. One cricket. Omar slowly moves the mug in front of the scarf's badge, as if that helps.*

[L053] LISANDRO: I knew it.
[L054] NARRATOR: Omar Berrada joined United from Manchester City. Lisandro had not done the reading.

## SCENE 7 — THE SPY WHO SPIED ME

**Guide time: 03:15–03:40.**

**INT. CARRINGTON. PLAYERS' LOUNGE. NIGHT.**

[L055] NARRATOR: That night, Lisandro Martinez believed he was the last loyal man at Carrington.

*Licho is alone. He looks over his shoulder, closes the blinds and locks the door. He takes out his phone: Alexis Mac Allister scores for Liverpool. Licho kisses the screen.*

[L056] LISANDRO: Vamos, Alexis. Vamos, hermano.

*Behind him, in the corner, is a pot plant that was not there before. It has trainers. The leaves part: Wilcox, still in the Leeds shirt.*

[L057] WILCOX: I knew it!

*Both scream. The plant falls over.*

[L058] LISANDRO: It's Argentina! It's different!
[L059] WILCOX: He plays for Liverpool, Lisandro!

*Beat.*

[L060] LISANDRO: It's a red shirt.

*FREEZE FRAME: the two of them pointing at each other.*

[L061] NARRATOR: Next time, on All or Something: Sir Jim, and his other football club.

**EXT. HEDGE OUTSIDE SIR JIM'S OFFICE. DAY.**

*Two hedges now, side by side. Both have binoculars. One is drinking mate. The other is drinking tea from a white mug.*

[L062] LISANDRO: Shhh. We are not here.
[L063] WILCOX: We're not here.

*END CARD: ALL OR SOMETHING.*
