## Animation and compositing direction

Use the existing rigged assets wherever they already move well. Cut individual figures and objects out of the supplied transparent sheets; do not place whole sheets into the video. Normalize character head size, outfit and ground height before swapping poses. The sheets preserve the visual design but are not mechanically identical rigs. Some performance poses switch microphone hands: preserve one chosen hand in each continuous shot, or make the transfer visible. New supporters have smiling mouths that should be cleaned to a neutral base if they need replacement lip sync.

For Rooney's close-ups, erase the baked base mouth before adding a new mouth. Track the mouth to the head, using separate anchors for front, three-quarter and profile views. Match skin edges and shading, constrain the mouth inside the lower-face region and keep chin volume stable. Teeth/tongue/mouth interior replace the original mouth rather than sit in an opaque rectangle on top. Align visemes to the source vocal, use closed shapes during pauses and hold open shapes through sustained vowels. Do not claim the timing is solved until it has been inspected against audio at full speed.

Parent the mic to the gripping hand. Keep its grille near the mouth during sung phrases without hiding every expression. Match a prop's front/behind order to fingers and torso. Give guitarists a visible fretting hand and a separate strumming wrist. For Maguire, place the body behind the drum kit, hands above the appropriate drums and sticks in the hands; strike on actual hits and rebound. Avoid permanent crossed sticks in front of his face.

Lock the planted walking foot in world space during contact; translate the hips over it. Add passing poses and alternating heel/toe lifts. Move the character horizontally only at a speed the stride can support. Add small separate contact shadows in the scene. For the overhead kick, shadow and ball trajectory must connect takeoff, strike and landing to the same ground plane.

Build backgrounds, contact shadows, characters, instruments, foreground crowd and effects as separate layers. Background PNGs are flattened plates, not pre-separated parallax layers. Reuse existing layer assets for depth if available. Fix the early pub speaker/monitor crossing Rio by assigning it a plausible position and depth; do not simply hide body defects under foreground objects. Keep the audience out of the character's foot-contact plane in street shots.

Use direct cuts, action matches and occasional short whip transitions. Cut at a lyric change, phrase ending, musical accent or reaction payoff. Faster passages can use one- or two-beat inserts; slower lines can hold several seconds. Do not cut on every beat or continuously zoom the same front shot. A new view should reveal a new action, perspective or reaction.

The plates contain a consistent red/amber base light. Add moving beams, rim light and beat-sensitive washes independently during animation. Keep face illumination readable, especially on Roy's dark outfit. Cool rooftop/tunnel contrast gives the warm stage return impact. Save maximum effects density for the end.
