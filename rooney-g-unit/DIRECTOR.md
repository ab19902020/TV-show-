# Director Script — Rooney & Rio at the 50 Cent Concert

**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical/mobile short  
**Runtime:** approximately 36.5 seconds  
**Audio:** use the original Rooney recording unchanged from start to finish  
**Art lock:** match the newly selected Rooney, Rio Ferdinand and 50 Cent character sheets, using the uploaded Bruno Fernandes / Jim Ratcliffe / Wayne Rooney sheets as the visual reference. Use the same bold outlines, big round eyes, compact body proportions, cel shading and phoneme mouth system for every character and background. No style drift, stretched bodies, or floating hands or microphones.

## Non-negotiable story rule
The Rooney recording is the entire spoken story. **Do not invent or add dialogue for 50 Cent, Rio Ferdinand, crew, crowd, or Rooney.** During the concert reenactment, the characters act silently to Rooney’s narration. The only on-stage lip-sync moment is Rooney’s recorded **“G-G-G-G-G-UNIT”** punchline, timed exactly to the source audio.

## Final staging — corrected version
50 Cent is **already performing on the MEN Arena stage** when the memory sequence reaches side-stage. Rooney and Rio are together in the wings. 50 Cent notices them while performing, looks across, and gives a **silent beckoning/come-on gesture** inviting them onto the stage. He does not speak to them and there is no audible “welcome to the stage.”

Rooney and Rio look at each other with a funny, uncertain reaction. They begin walking on. As they enter, **50 Cent walks offstage**, leaving Rooney and Rio exposed in front of the huge crowd. They look at each other again, then at the audience. Rooney and Rio each receive a handheld microphone before walking on. Rooney looks down at his microphone, gets the bad idea, walks to the front with growing confidence, raises his microphone, and delivers **“G-G-G-G-G-UNIT”** exactly on the original audio.

**Latest staging correction:** Rooney and Rio must visibly have a handheld microphone each, matching the recording. Both hold them securely before the walk-on. Rooney delivers the punchline into his own handheld microphone. Rio keeps his microphone through the laughing reaction. There is no stand microphone in this corrected edit.

# Shot-by-shot director timeline

## 0:00–0:03 — Memory begins
**Audio:** Rooney begins the story about getting on stage with 50 Cent.

**Visual:** hard cut from the podcast/current setup into the memory. Quick portrait establishing shot of the MEN Arena exterior at night, then a fast push/whip inside.

**Camera:** short 0.4–0.6 second push-in, then cut straight into the concert atmosphere.

## 0:03–0:06 — Rooney and Rio are side-stage
**Visual:** reveal Rooney and Rio together in the wings. Through the opening behind them, 50 Cent is already performing on the live stage.

**Acting:** Rooney is excited and slightly loose from the night out. Rio is calmer and gives Rooney a sideways look that says this could go badly.

**Important:** nobody visibly speaks. Rooney’s recording remains narration.

## 0:06–0:10 — “we’d both had a few drinks” energy
**Visual:** Rooney leans slightly toward the stage, overconfident. Rio steadies him with a light shoulder/arm touch or gives him a slow warning look.

**Comedy:** restrained, not slapstick drunk. One tiny balance correction is enough.

**Camera:** medium two-shot so feet/body posture read clearly. No sliding feet.

## 0:10–0:14 — 50 Cent notices them
**Visual:** cut to 50 Cent performing. He glances toward the side-stage entrance, recognises Rooney/Rio, then gives a confident grin.

**Action:** without speaking, he points/beckons them toward the stage while continuing the performance energy.

**Camera:** medium shot of 50 Cent with the wings visible enough to establish eye-line.

## 0:14–0:18 — Silent invitation / funny Rooney-Rio look
**Visual:** back to Rooney and Rio. Both register that 50 Cent is inviting them on.

**Reaction sequence:**
1. Rooney looks at Rio.
2. Rio looks at Rooney.
3. Both look at 50 Cent.
4. Rooney gives a small “why not?” grin/nod.
5. Rio gives a slower, less convinced look.

**Comedy:** let the eye contact breathe. No dialogue and no exaggerated mouth movement.

## 0:18–0:22 — They enter; 50 Cent exits
**Visual:** Rooney and Rio step onto the main stage. At almost the same moment, 50 Cent casually moves past/around them and walks off toward the wing.

**Acting:**
- 50 Cent: amused glance, tiny smirk, effortless exit.
- Rooney: confidence freezes for a fraction as he realises 50 has gone.
- Rio: turns to watch 50 Cent disappear.

**Camera:** wide enough to show all three and the crowd scale.

## 0:22–0:25 — “What’s going on here?” beat
**Visual:** reverse angle from behind Rooney and Rio to the massive MEN Arena crowd, then back to their faces.

**Facial gag:**
- Rio looks at Rooney.
- Rooney looks at Rio.
- both look at the crowd.
- Rio looks back at Rooney a second time, slower.

**Important:** this is silent acting under the narration. Do not make their mouths speak the narrated words.

## 0:25–0:29 — Rooney sees the microphone
**Visual:** Rooney’s eyes drift away from Rio and land on the handheld microphone he has been given.

**Acting:** his expression changes from uncertainty to a mischievous “I’ve got an idea” grin. Rio notices Rooney looking at the mic and immediately realises what is about to happen.

**Camera:** Rooney close-up → insert of mic → Rio reaction.

## 0:29–0:33 — Rooney walks to the mic
**Visual:** Rooney walks toward the front of the stage with his microphone in hand with absurdly growing confidence, like he now thinks he is headlining the show. Rio stays several steps behind.

**Comedy:** Rooney glances back once. Rio gives a small “do not do this” head shake. Rooney takes it as encouragement and turns back to the mic.

**Camera:** low/medium tracking shot; keep feet planted correctly and microphones firmly held in their hands.

## 0:33–0:36.5 — “G-G-G-G-G-UNIT” punchline
**Visual:** Rooney raises his own microphone and delivers the recorded line with complete commitment.

**Lip-sync:** this is the one concert moment that must be frame-accurate to the Rooney source audio. Use the established viseme system. Each “G” gets a small, precise head/mic pulse; do not bounce or warp the whole body. Hit “UNIT” cleanly, then hold the triumphant pose for a fraction.

**Reaction chain:**
1. Rio goes blank for a tiny beat.
2. Rio folds into laughter.
3. 50 Cent can briefly glance back from the wing, amused.
4. Crowd reaction swells visually.
5. Rooney looks extremely pleased with himself.

**End:** freeze/hold for a fraction on Rooney proud at the mic with Rio laughing behind, then cut to black exactly with the end of the original audio.

# Animation and polish rules
- Original Rooney audio is untouched and remains the entire spoken soundtrack.
- No added dialogue, announcer line, crowd speech, or 50 Cent speech.
- Concert-memory Rooney does **not** lip-sync the narration; only the quoted “G-G-G-G-G-UNIT” moment is visibly lip-synced on stage.
- Use the existing A-E-I-O-U/phoneme workflow and consonant shapes. Lead a mouth pose by a frame only where the established rig requires it.
- Natural blinks, half-blinks, eye darts and reaction holds carry the comedy. Avoid constant movement.
- Rooney/Rio eye contact is a recurring joke and should be readable even without sound.
- No body stretching or image warping to fake motion. Use proper cutout poses/rigging.
- Feet stay planted; no skating/sliding walk cycles.
- Microphone must stay physically anchored to its stand/hand as appropriate; no floating mic.
- Stage lighting can move, but character exposure must remain stable and not flicker.
- Foreground speakers/monitors should create real occlusion when characters cross behind them.
- All new artwork is 9:16 portrait/mobile-first and should still read cleanly when viewed on a phone.
- Final render may be 4K vertical; previews can be 720p vertical.

# Quality target
The short must feel like it belongs inside the exact same animated universe as the approved Micah Richards / Rooney build: identical character finish and facial language, restrained acting between punchlines, accurate stage geography, and comedy driven by timing and reactions rather than random movement.

# Visual polish for the approved cartoon version
- Rio keeps one complete native head for all reactions: animate eyes, lids, brows and the closed mouth on that drawing; never lay a second facial-expression drawing over it.
- Close-ups omit distracting partial slices of the other character.
- 50 Cent passes behind Rooney and Rio on a different stage depth.
- Full microphone-holding drawings carry the hands and props together through walks and close-ups. The raised microphone is protected from Rooney’s jaw and head animation.
- Retain the approved cartoon backgrounds, selected character identities, complete original recording and 36.5-second edit.
